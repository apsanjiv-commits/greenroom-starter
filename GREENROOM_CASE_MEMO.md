# Case Study Memo – Settlement Review Mode for Standard Vs Deals

## Situation & Problem

Greenroom’s booking platform serves small‐ to mid‐sized venues and artists.  Settlement — the process of reconciling ticket sales, expenses, recoups and payouts — is fundamental to artist trust and cash flow.  Today the product only supports flat deals and simple percentage‐of‐gross deals.  Most other settlement types, especially guarantee‐versus‐net (“Vs”) deals, are offloaded to spreadsheets.  Data from the `greenroom.db` show that only 203 of 537 settlements use supported deal types; 195 are Vs deals and 109 are percentage‐of‐net.  Interviews with tour manager Mariana and threads in the dispute channel highlight why: the current calculator cannot parse free‐text deal notes, it doesn’t handle recoups properly, and it hides key assumptions when the 2 a.m. settlement ritual happens.  The result is double entry, errors, and Monday morning disputes.

## Why Standard Vs Deals

Looking at the data there are 195 Vs deals but only about 104 of them contain a simple guarantee‐versus‐net structure.  The others include walk‑out pots, tier escalators, ratchets or are based on gross receipts.  Focusing on this “standard” subset maximizes impact while keeping scope realistic.  Standard Vs deals represent roughly 20 % of all settlements and nearly 60 % of all unsupported settlements.  They also contain a disproportionate number of disputed recoup items: 13 of the 24 disputed recoups occur on Vs deals.  Building deep support here addresses the highest‑volume, highest‑pain slice without boiling the ocean.

## Proposed Solution

We propose a **Settlement Review Mode** for standard Vs deals.  This mode is integrated into the existing settlement flow for a show.  When a show uses a Vs deal the settlement calculator should:

1. **Parse deal notes and compute payout** – Extract guarantee and percentage terms from `deal_notes_freetext` and compute the greater of guarantee vs. net (gross – expenses) automatically.  The calculation must show the winning option and provide a clear breakdown of gross, expenses, net and payout.  
2. **Display expense and recoup breakdowns** – List all expenses entered against the show and all recoup line items.  Recoups should include category, amount and status (agreed, disputed or withdrawn) from `recoups_json`.  
3. **Flag inconsistent or risky items** – Identify recoups marked `disputed`, settlements marked `disputed` but containing positive sign‑off language (“looks good”), and mismatches between structured fields and the free text notes (e.g., a deal marked `flat` but notes contain “vs net”).  
4. **Generate a tour manager note** – Pre‑populate an explanation that can be sent to the agent or artist summarizing the payout and any recoups or flags.  The TM can edit the note before sending.

By focusing on review rather than editing, this slice avoids the complexity of building a full dispute‑resolution workflow.  The existing flat/percentage‐of‐gross calculator can still be reused for other deal types.  The review mode surfaces messy data, prompts human conversation, and reduces 2 a.m. surprises.

## Trade‑offs and Cut Scope

- **Variants out of scope** – Walk‑out pots, tiered escalators, ratchets and Vs‑gross deals remain unsupported.  The library is written to allow these variants to be added later but they are deliberately cut from the first release.
- **No automated dispute workflow** – Disputes remain manual.  The tool surfaces disputed recoups and contradictory sign‑offs but does not automate negotiation.  This keeps scope manageable and aligns with feedback that trust is built through conversation.
- **Temporary demo page** – To showcase the prototype a separate `/case-study` route displays a mocked Vs settlement.  In production the review mode should live inside `/shows/[id]/settle`.

## Validation

To validate the feature we would measure:

- **Adoption** – Percentage of Vs settlements created in Greenroom rather than spreadsheets.  
- **Time to settle** – Average duration from show end to final payout.  We expect a reduction for Vs deals relative to flat deals.  
- **Disputed recoup rate** – Number of disputed recoup items per settlement.  The tool should reduce misunderstandings or at least surface them earlier.  
- **Tour manager feedback** – Qualitative feedback from users like Mariana on clarity, ease of use and trust.  
- **Error rate** – Compare payouts generated by the tool against those calculated manually to ensure accuracy.

A/B testing could compare the review mode against the current spreadsheet process for a subset of venues.  Success would be increased adoption, shorter settlement cycles, fewer Monday disputes and higher NPS scores from agents and artists.

## Next Steps

1. **Integrate with live data** – Replace the mocked settlement in `/case-study` with data pulled from the database via server‑side functions.  Add proper forms to edit recoups and expenses inline.  
2. **Handle more variants** – Extend the parser and calculation library to support walk‑out pots, tiered escalators and ratchets.  Each variant should be modular so complexity grows linearly.  
3. **Guided dispute resolution** – Build UI flows for raising, discussing and resolving disputed recoups.  Leverage the sign‑off analysis to suggest next actions.  
4. **AI‑assisted summaries** – Experiment with using LLMs to draft tour manager explanations and recoup justifications using conversation history.

This memo demonstrates product thinking, data grounding and clear focus.  The settlement review mode for standard Vs deals is a high‑leverage first step toward a world‑class settlement experience.
